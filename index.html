Common questions

What is permanent jewelry?

Permanent jewelry is a custom-fit chain welded closed on your wrist, ankle, or neck instead of using a clasp. You pick the chain, I fit it to you exactly, and a quick painless spark seals it. You wear it 24/7.

How much does permanent jewelry cost?

Bracelets start at $XX, anklets at $XX, and necklaces at $XX. The price covers your chain, custom fitting, and the weld. Charms are extra.

Does the weld hurt?

Not at all. The spark never touches your skin. Most people say they barely notice it.

Can I take it off later?

Yes. You can snip it off anytime with scissors, and I can re-weld it back on for you later.

How long does an appointment take?

About 15 to 20 minutes for one piece. Bring friends and make it a thing, groups are my favorite.

Will it set off airport security or interfere with MRIs?

It won't set off airport metal detectors. For an MRI you'll need to remove it first, then come back and I'll weld it right back on.

Can I shower and swim with it?

Yes. Permanent jewelry is made to be worn 24/7, through showers, workouts, and swimming. No taking it on and off.

What if my chain breaks?

Bring it back and I'll re-weld it for you. Chains rarely break in normal wear, but if it happens, you're covered.

Do you do parties and group events?

Yes, and they're my favorite. Girls' nights, bridal parties, mother-daughter dates, team events. Text me and we'll set it up.
